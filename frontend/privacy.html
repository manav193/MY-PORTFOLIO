Privacy

This portfolio is designed to collect as little visitor data as practical. The sections below explain what is stored, where it is stored, and what leaves the browser.

NIMO

NIMO may keep recent prompts, language preference, onboarding state, and interface context in your browser using sessionStorage or localStorage. This supports continuity and does not require an account. Local intent handling stays in the browser. Requests that need NIMO Core may send the prompt, bounded conversation context, current public page identifier, and language to the public NIMO Worker.

ArcadeOS and local preferences

ArcadeOS stores settings such as boot mode, sound preference, reduced-motion preference, favorites, recent app state, and game progress in localStorage. This data remains on the current browser unless you clear site data or use an in-product reset.

Portfolio analytics

No active Google Analytics or Microsoft Clarity tracking is intentionally enabled in the current public build. Standard hosting infrastructure may still process basic request metadata such as IP address, user agent, timestamp, and requested URL for security, reliability, and delivery.

Contact form

The contact form sends the name, reply email, and message you provide to the portfolio's serverless contact endpoint. When email delivery is configured, the endpoint forwards the message through Resend to the portfolio owner. If delivery is unavailable, the site opens your email application instead and does not claim that a message was sent.

Third-party services

Public project links may open GitHub, Vercel-hosted products, Cloudflare Workers, Google Fonts, or other explicitly linked services. Their own privacy policies apply after you leave this portfolio.

Your controls

You can clear local portfolio data through browser site settings, private browsing, or available reset controls. You can also block optional browser permissions such as microphone access. The core portfolio remains usable without microphone or gamepad access.
