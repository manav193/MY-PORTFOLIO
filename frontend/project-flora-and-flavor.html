Flora & Flavor

Botanical dining, reimagined through cinematic motion and seasonal storytelling.

01. Vision

A restaurant site that feels alive.

The concept avoids a conventional menu-first landing page. Instead, the user enters through a cinematic hero where scrolling rotates and scales a plated bowl while typography arrives in sequence, turning the brand story into a controlled visual performance.

02. Experience

Editorial, useful, responsive.

  • GSAP and ScrollTrigger pinned hero choreography
  • Scroll-synchronised bowl rotation and scale
  • Staggered heading, copy and CTA reveals
  • Seasonal vegetarian menu with filters
  • Reservation validation and confirmation feedback
  • Gallery, reviews, location and order interactions
  • Mobile navigation and responsive layouts
  • Reduced-motion support
03. Design

Elegant botanical language.

Deep natural surfaces, warm cream typography and restrained botanical accents establish a premium vegetarian identity. Large editorial spacing gives photography and motion enough room to breathe while clear controls preserve usability.

04. Challenge

Motion without emptiness.

The hero initially centered only on the bowl, leaving the experience visually sparse. The final interaction uses scroll position to bring the story, actions and product composition together rather than preloading text or fading it away.

05. Outcome

A portfolio-ready hospitality concept.

The result demonstrates cinematic frontend choreography, restaurant UX, responsive design and accessible motion in a dependency-light static implementation.
