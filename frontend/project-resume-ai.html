01. Problem

Resume creation is structurally difficult.

Job seekers often struggle to organize experience, write concise summaries, choose relevant skills and preserve a professional format. Generic document editors provide freedom but little guidance.

02. Product

Guided mobile workflow.

  • Structured resume builder
  • AI-assisted summary writing
  • Skills suggestions
  • ATS-oriented template direction
  • Live resume preview
  • PDF-export goal
  • Search and resume management
  • Dark mode and Material 3 UI
03. Architecture

Native Android foundation.

The documented product direction uses Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, Navigation Compose, MVVM and Room for local resume storage. The architecture separates UI state, persistence and future AI-service integration.

04. UX Principle

Guidance without lock-in.

AI suggestions should accelerate writing while leaving the user in control of every claim. Templates should protect hierarchy and ATS readability without making every resume look identical.

05. Status

Concept documentation.

The product capabilities and Android stack are documented; final screenshots, build verification and implemented-versus-planned feature confirmation still need repository-level validation.
